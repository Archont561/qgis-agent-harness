## ORDINANCE NO. 3

### OF THE MINISTER OF DEVELOPMENT, LABOUR AND TECHNOLOGY [1)]

of 4 December 2020

**on the appointment of a Team to assess degree theses, doctoral dissertations, publications and innovative solutions in the field of geoinformation in the Competition for the Minister of Development, Labour and Technology Award, on laying down the Rules of Work of that Team, and on laying down the Competition Rules**

Warsaw, 7 December 2020

Pursuant to Art. 7(4)(5) of the Act of 8 August 1996 on the Council of Ministers, the following is ordained:

**§ 1.** 1. A Team is appointed to assess degree theses, doctoral dissertations, publications and innovative solutions in the field of geoinformation (the "Team") in the Competition for the Minister of Development, Labour and Technology Award for the best degree theses, doctoral dissertations, publications and innovative solutions in the field of geoinformation (the "Minister's Award Competition").

2. The Team consists of:

1. the Chair of the Team,
2. the Deputy Chair of the Team,
3. Members of the Team, numbering 8 to 12,
4. the Secretary of the Team

— appointed by the Minister of Development, Labour and Technology (the "Minister").

3. Members of the Team need not be employees of the Ministry of Development, Labour and Technology.

**§ 2.** 1. The Team's task is the assessment of degree theses, doctoral dissertations, publications and innovative solutions in the field of geoinformation in the Minister's Award Competition.

2. The decision on the award of prizes is taken by the Minister, on the basis of the protocol and recommendations referred to in § 2(2) of annex 1.

3. No appeal lies against the Minister's decision.

**§ 3.** The Team acts on the basis of the Rules of Work of the Team, constituting annex 1 to this Ordinance, and the Rules of the Minister's Award Competition, constituting annex 2.

**§ 4.** Organisational and technical support for the Team is provided by the Department of Architecture, Construction and Geodesy of the Ministry (the "DAB").

**§ 5.** 1. Membership of the Team and participation in its work are unpaid.

2. Where sittings are held outside the members' place of residence, members who attended are entitled to a refund of travel costs:

1. by public collective transport against the attached tickets, and in the case of rail travel at the price of a second-class ticket;
2. by private car, against a declaration accepted by the Director of the DAB or a person authorised by him, at the rate set out in the regulations issued under Art. 34a(2) of the Act of 6 September 2001 on road transport.

3. Travel refunds are settled within the funds in the DAB's financial plan.

**§ 6.** The Ordinance of the Minister of Development of 5 October 2020 on the appointment of a Team to assess degree theses, doctoral dissertations and habilitation dissertations, publications and innovative solutions in the field of geoinformation in the Competition for the Minister of Development Award, on laying down the Rules of Work of that Team and the Competition Rules, ceases to be in force.

**§ 7.** Applications submitted and not decided before this Ordinance enters into force are governed by this Ordinance.

**§ 8.** This Ordinance enters into force on the day following its announcement.

# Annex 1 — RULES OF WORK OF THE TEAM

**§ 1.** 1. The Team performs its tasks at sittings and takes decisions by resolution. Sittings may also be held by means of electronic communication.

2. Resolutions are taken by a simple majority in the presence of at least half of the Members. In the event of a tie, the Chair's vote decides.

**§ 2.** 1. The Chair's tasks are:

1. directing the Team's work;
2. setting the dates of sittings and notifying the Members;
3. chairing the sittings;
4. presenting to the Minister a protocol containing the results of the Team's work and its recommendations on the award of prizes, for the taking of the final decision;
5. resolving disputed questions arising in the course of the Team's work;
6. representing the Team externally;
7. performing other tasks arising from the Team's remit;
8. appointing, where necessary, problem sub-teams from among the Members.

2. By 31 January each year the Chair presents to the Minister, in the form of a protocol, the results of the Team's work and its recommendations with justifications for the awards.

**§ 3.** 1. The Deputy Chair's tasks are:

1. the qualification of applications and the preparation of the substantive justification of the theses, dissertations, publications or innovative solutions proposed for an award;
2. directing the Team's work, including convening and chairing sittings, in the Chair's absence.

2. The Secretary's task is the formal check of applications and of the submitted theses, dissertations, publications and innovative solutions.

3. The remaining Members' tasks are set by the Chair during sittings.

**§ 4.** 1. The assessment referred to in § 2(1) of the Ordinance is made on the basis of applications submitted in the manner set out in the Rules of the Minister's Award Competition, in three stages:

1. **stage I** — a formal assessment of the applications, theses, dissertations, publications and innovative solutions, made by the Secretary;
2. **stage II** — a substantive assessment of the submitted theses, dissertations, publications and innovative solutions, made by the Deputy Chair;
3. **stage III** — the final assessment of the submitted works and the drafting of recommendations for the Minister, made by the Team.

2. The works submitted for an award are assessed against the following criteria:

1. **degree theses:**
   1. the creative value of the study,
   2. the degree of difficulty of the thesis,
   3. the originality and modernity of the solution,
   4. the clarity of the formulation of the aim, the way it is achieved, and the correctness of the conclusions and of the form of presentation,
   5. the practical value of the thesis;
2. **doctoral dissertations:**
   1. the scientific value of the study (the topicality of the subject),
   2. the originality of methods and solutions,
   3. the degree of difficulty of the work (theoretical and experimental),
   4. the significance of the results for science and technology, and concrete gains for economic practice;
3. **domestic publications or foreign publications translated into Polish:**
   1. creative or documentary values making a substantial contribution to science or technology (topicality and originality of a popularising publication),
   2. the selection and presentation of the subject, facilitating the introduction of solutions into practice or teaching,
   3. editorial, publishing, graphic, linguistic and other merits justifying an award;
4. **innovative solutions:**
   1. the originality, inventiveness and creative value of the solution,
   2. usefulness,
   3. the significance of the results for the development of Polish science and technology,
   4. the possibility of a universal implementation of the solution.

**§ 5.** The Chair decides on the exclusion from the Team's work, for the given year, of a Member who is the author or co-author of a work submitted for an award that year, or where other grounds for a lack of impartiality exist. Exclusion applies to the category in which the work was submitted.

# Annex 2 — RULES OF THE MINISTER'S AWARD COMPETITION

## Chapter 1 — General provisions

**§ 1.** 1. These Rules set out the procedure for awarding prizes in the Minister's Award Competition.

2. Prizes are awarded annually in the following categories and according to the following rules:

1. engineering theses — at most 2 prizes in the form of statuettes;
2. master's theses — at most 4 prizes in the form of statuettes;
3. doctoral dissertations — at most 2 prizes in the form of statuettes;
4. domestic publications — at most 1 prize in the form of a statuette;
5. innovative solutions — at most 2 prizes in the form of statuettes.

3. If in a given year fewer prizes are awarded in a category than set out in para. 2, the Minister, on the Team's motion, may award more prizes in another category.

4. Prizes are financed from the funds of the Ministry of Development, Labour and Technology.

## Chapter 2 — Participation

**§ 2.** 1. The following may be entered for the Minister's Award Competition:

1. degree theses and doctoral dissertations — defended within the two years preceding the submission of the application;
2. publications, in the form of printed books, published within the two years preceding the application;
3. innovative solutions — devices, products, methods or technological processes implemented and operating on the market, whose use began no more than 3 years earlier.

2. The Competition is open to Polish citizens and to foreigners who have defended degree theses or doctoral dissertations, published works, or implemented innovative solutions in geoinformation in Poland.

3. Entry requires the submission of an application to the Minister, through the DAB, in the template constituting the annexes to these Rules, according to category.

4. Applications may be filed by:

1. for degree theses — rectors, senates or the bodies of higher-education institutions referred to in Art. 28(4) of the Act of 20 July 2018 — Law on Higher Education and Science, or other non-university organisational units entitled to confer academic degrees;
2. for doctoral dissertations — rectors, senates or the bodies of higher-education institutions entitled to confer the doctoral degree, or other non-university organisational units entitled to confer the doctoral degree;
3. for publications — publishers, and organisational units and institutions employing the authors or using the publications in their activity during the period covered by the application;
4. for innovative solutions — creators, organisational units and natural or legal persons connected with the creation or use of the submitted innovative solution.

**§ 3.** The following are attached to the application:

1. for prizes for degree theses:
   1. a review of the thesis written by its reviewer or supervisor,
   2. an electronic copy of the thesis;
2. for prizes for doctoral dissertations:
   1. the opinion of the applying unit,
   2. at least two reviews of the dissertation, prepared by persons holding a habilitation or a professorial title, representing the same field,
   3. a copy of the document confirming the conferral of the doctoral degree, signed by the body competent to confer it under the rules in force on the day of conferral,
   4. an electronic copy of the dissertation;
3. for publications:
   1. at least two opinions or publishing reviews,
   2. an electronic copy of the publication;
4. for innovative solutions:
   1. a description of the innovative solution indicating its originality, inventiveness and creative value, its usefulness, its significance for the development of Polish science and technology, and the possibility of a universal implementation,
   2. a statement confirming the implementation and market operation of the solution during the three years preceding the application,
   3. at least one user opinion or reference concerning the use of the innovative solution.

## Chapter 3 — Submission procedure

**§ 4.** Applications with the documents referred to in § 3 are to be submitted **by 30 November each year** to one of the following addresses:

1. Ministry of Development, Labour and Technology, Department of Architecture, Construction and Geodesy, Chałubińskiego 4/6, 00-926 Warsaw,
2. the main office of the Ministry, Pl. Trzech Krzyży 3/5, 00-507 Warsaw,
3. sekretariatDAB@mrpit.gov.pl,
4. ePUAP: /MPIT/SkrytkaESP

— marked on the envelope or in the subject line "Konkurs o Nagrodę Ministra – wybitne osiągnięcia naukowe i innowacyjne rozwiązania w dziedzinie geoinformacji".

**§ 5.** 1. Applications submitted after the deadline in § 4 will not be considered; the date of receipt decides.

2. Where an application arrives within the deadline but fails formal requirements — a missing or illegible copy of the thesis or publication, a missing copy of the document confirming the doctoral degree, missing opinions or publishing reviews, a missing consent of the author(s) to the entry — the Secretary calls on the applicant, by e-mail, to remedy the defects within a set period of not less than 7 days, with a warning that failure to do so will result in the rejection of the application for non-compliance with formal requirements.

3. Works submitted that do not win the Minister's Award are returned to the applicants, after the Team has finished and the results are announced, at the correspondence address given in the application; if no address is given, the works may be destroyed.

4. The announcement of the next edition of the Competition is published on the Ministry's website.

## Chapter 4 — Award ceremony

**§ 6.** 1. Laureates will be informed of the results and of the date and place of the award ceremony.

2. The information about the results and the ceremony will be available on the Ministry's website.

## GDPR information clause (identical in annexes 1–4)

> In the original the clause appears under each of the four applications in identical wording; the full version follows, once. The special-category text below is the Polish ministry's standard clause and refers to the ordinance cited in point 3.

Pursuant to Art. 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (GDPR), please be informed that:

1. The controller of your personal data is the Minister of Development, Labour and Technology, with its seat in Warsaw, Plac Trzech Krzyży 3/5, 00-507 Warsaw, kancelaria@mrpit.gov.pl.
2. The data protection officer is available at IOD@mrpit.gov.pl.
3. Your personal data will be processed on the basis of Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR, that is, processing to fulfil a legal obligation incumbent on the controller in connection with Ordinance no. 3 of the Minister of Development, Labour and Technology of 4 December 2020.
4. Your personal data will be processed in order to:
   1. take part in the Minister's Award Competition,
   2. publish the laureates on the Ministry's website, in the event of an award.
5. Your personal data may be disclosed to other recipients or categories of recipients, in particular the members of the Team appointed to assess the works. Information on the results and the award ceremony will also be available on the Ministry's website.
6. Providing your personal data is voluntary but necessary to take part in the competition.
7. Your data will be processed until the purpose referred to in point 4 is realised, and no longer than the provisions of the Act of 14 July 1983 on the national archival resource and archives provide.
8. In connection with the processing you have the right to:
   1. request access to your personal data under Art. 15 GDPR,
   2. request rectification of your personal data under Art. 16 GDPR,
   3. request restriction of processing under Art. 18 GDPR,
   4. lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office if you consider that the processing infringes GDPR (Art. 77 GDPR).
9. Your personal data will not be subject to automated decision-making, including profiling.
10. Your personal data will not be transferred to a third country.
