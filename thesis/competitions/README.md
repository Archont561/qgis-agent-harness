# Competitions for degree theses — the rulebooks (converted to Markdown)

Three independent competitions, three different rulebooks. The files below are **faithful conversions of the sources** (a web page and two PDFs), with the § numbering and the annexes preserved so that cross-references still resolve. They are working copies: **anything quoted in the thesis or in an application is quoted from the original source**, not from this directory.

| File | Competition | Organiser | The "master's thesis" category | Intake |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`pti-kpm-rules.md`](pti-kpm-rules.md) | the Master's Thesis Competition (KPM) | Polish Information Processing Society (PTI), national board on the motion of the Lower Silesian branch | master's theses **in computing** | entries **by 10 October** of the year of the announcement; results **by the end of the calendar year** |
| [`sgp-kd-rules-2023.md`](sgp-kd-rules-2023.md) | the competition for the best engineering and master's theses | Association of Polish Surveyors (SGP) + the Surveyor General of Poland | master's theses in **geodesy and cartography** or related fields, grade **very good** | dates given in the announcement (§8); entries through `konkurs.sgp.geodezja.org.pl` |
| [`marpit-geoinformation-award-2020.md`](marpit-geoinformation-award-2020.md) | the competition for the Minister of Development, Labour and Technology Award | the Minister (administered by DAB) | master's theses in **geoinformation** — at most four statuettes a year | applications **by 30 November** each year; the thesis defended within the **two years** preceding the application |

## What follows for this thesis

- **PTI KPM** — the greatest visibility (a national, widely commented competition), but the condition is substantive: the thesis has to *be about computing*. This thesis (a comparison of agent interfaces, MCP, PyQGIS) qualifies **if** the frame is computing and GIS is the application domain, not the other way round. That is an argument for how chapters 1 and 3 are set up (see `framing/scope-and-character.md`).
- **SGP + the Surveyor General** — the closest in subject (geodesy and cartography), but it requires a **very good** grade and a platform entry; the deadline depends on the edition, so the announcement has to be watched.
- **The Minister's Award** — the geoinformation category, the highest rank, but **the application is filed by the university** (rector/senate/body), not by the author. In practice: the dean's office has to be persuaded and a review has to exist. The deadline is 30 November with a two-year window after the defence — the decision therefore comes after graduation.

**The common denominator:** all three assess "scientific level / originality / practical value / editorial form". Those are exactly the axes the faculty's regulations list — so ordering the thesis for an examiner works for all three competitions at once. Nothing has to be written "for a competition".

## Editorial notes on the conversion

- The PDFs were set in two columns with hyphenated word breaks; every such break (`organizacy j-nych`, `w y-drukowane`, `pot wierdzające` and so on) has been joined, with the content and numbering untouched.
- Forms (applications, certificates, consents) are reproduced as `text` blocks with the field layout preserved.
- The GDPR clause in annexes 1–4 of the Minister's Award rules is **identical four times** in the original — included once, with an explicit note.
- Only typographic furniture of the print was dropped: the footers ("Dziennik Urzędowy … – n – Poz. 6") and the page with the minister's signature.
- The PTI's personal-data notice (`/terms-rodo`) is an **integral part** of the PTI rules (§5.7) — appended at the end of the PTI file.

## A note on this translation

The three conversions were originally prepared in Polish, as verbatim conversions of Polish regulations. They are now in English so that the whole repository reads in one language. **The article numbers are unchanged**, so every reference of the form "SGP §11" keeps working, and the Polish originals remain the source of record: when a rule is quoted in the thesis, in a competition application or in a complaint, quote it from the official document, not from here. Where a Polish term has no settled English equivalent (KPM, SGP, GGK, `praca magisterska`), the Polish abbreviation is kept.
