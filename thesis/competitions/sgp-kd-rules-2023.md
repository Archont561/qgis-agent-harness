# Rules of the competition for the best engineering and master's theses (SGP + the Surveyor General)

> **Source:** the SGP + Surveyor General rules, 8 pages; the original PDF was removed on 2026-11-07 after conversion — see `../external-sources.md`. The conversion is faithful, with the § numbering and the annexes preserved. English translation 2026-11-07; the Polish original is the source of record, and anything quoted in an application is quoted from it.
> **Organisers:** the Association of Polish Surveyors, the Surveyor General of Poland
> **Adopted:** by the Presidium of the SGP national board at its sitting of 1 June 2023 (§17)
> **Submission platform:** konkurs.sgp.geodezja.org.pl

These Rules set out the aims, the subject matter, and the rules and procedure for organising, conducting and deciding the competition for the best engineering and master's theses (the "Competition"), written within full-time and part-time studies in all types of Polish higher-education institutions that run studies in the field of geodesy and cartography or related fields. The Competition is decided in two categories, separately for engineering theses and for master's theses.

## I. THE ORGANISER

### §1

The organisers of the Competition are:

1. the Association of Polish Surveyors,
2. the Surveyor General of Poland.

The Organiser appoints an Organising Committee responsible for all organisational matters connected with running the Competition, including in particular the coordination of the Competition Commission's work and contact with participants.

## II. AIM, SUBJECT MATTER, TOPICS AND FORM

### §2

1. The main aim of the Competition is to distinguish the best degree theses written within full-time and part-time studies in geodesy and cartography or related fields, characterised by the highest scientific level, practical potential and originality of solution.
2. The intermediate aims are:
   1. promoting the best degree theses,
   2. stimulating scientific, inventive and rationalising initiative,
   3. easing the professional start of the best graduates in geodesy and cartography or related fields.

### §3

The subject of the Competition are engineering or master's theses meeting the following conditions:

1. The thesis was written within full-time or part-time studies in geodesy and cartography or related fields.
2. The topic of the thesis comprises a study within one of the specialisations **in the area of geodesy and cartography**.
3. The thesis entered for the Competition was written in Polish or English.
4. The submitted thesis was graded **very good**.

### §4

The form of the Competition:

1. The Competition is open and cyclical.
2. It proceeds in a single stage.

## III. ADDRESSEES AND PARTICIPANTS

### §5

The Competition is open to graduates of geodesy and cartography or related fields, of full-time and part-time first- or second-cycle studies at Polish public and non-public higher-education institutions.

## IV. ENTRY, DEADLINES AND CONDITIONS OF PARTICIPATION

### §6

1. Theses may be entered in two ways:
   1. by the deans of faculties, on the motion of the faculty degree-examination commissions and with the consent of the author(s),
   2. individually, by the authors themselves.
2. Both individual and team theses may be entered; in team theses an equal percentage contribution of the authors is assumed.

### §7

1. Only theses defended in the given academic year to which the current edition of the Competition relates may be entered.
2. A thesis may be entered only once.
3. Entries are made entirely electronically through the platform at konkurs.sgp.geodezja.org.pl.
4. The template of the author's declarations and consents that must be accepted directly in the system during the entry process is annex 1 to these Rules.
5. All documents requiring the signature of the entrant or of third parties must be printed, signed and then scanned and attached to the entry.
6. The documents requiring a signature are:
   1. a certificate issued by the university authorities confirming the grade obtained for the thesis together with the date of defence (template — annex 2 to these Rules),
   2. consent to the processing and publication of the personal data of the thesis supervisor and, in the case of a prize-winning thesis, to the publication of the supervisor's name on the internet and in other media and promotional materials connected with the Competition. (The supervisor gives consent voluntarily. A lack of such consent does not exclude the thesis from the Competition; annex 3 to these Rules.)
7. Theses submitted without the required annexes, or with incomplete or untrue data, will not take part in the Competition.

### §8

1. The deadlines for entries and for the decision of the Competition are given in the announcement of the Competition.
2. Theses submitted after the deadline will not be considered.

### §9

1. The Competition Commission verifies whether the submitted theses correspond to the substantive scope of the Competition set out in §3(2).
2. Theses verified negatively will not take part in the Competition.

### §10

The Chair of the Organising Committee is authorised to give any information concerning the Competition on the Organiser's behalf.

## V. CRITERIA OF ASSESSMENT

### §11

1. In assessing the theses the Competition Commission takes the following criteria into account:
   1. the scientific level of the thesis and/or the practical significance of the problem posed and analysed in it,
   2. the degree of difficulty of the task undertaken,
   3. the way the problem is solved, and the innovativeness and originality of the proposed solutions (the novel and creative character of the thesis),
   4. the logical construction of the thesis,
   5. the form of presentation and its graphic design.
2. A thesis may obtain at most **40 points**, of which: 0–10 for each of the criteria in para. 1 points 1–3, and 0–5 for the criteria in para. 1 points 4–5.

## VI. RULES FOR SELECTING LAUREATES

### §12

1. The Competition Commission and its Chair are appointed by the Presidium of the SGP national board on the motion of the Main Commission for Professional Education.
2. The Competition Commission consists of academic teachers from at least four higher-education institutions educating in geodesy and cartography or related fields, and representatives of the geodetic and cartographic administration and industry.
3. The Commission carries out a preliminary formal assessment and, on the basis of the criteria in chapter V §11 paras 1–2, a substantive assessment, producing a ranking list.
4. The Commission selects the best theses on the basis of that ranking list. In the event of equal scores the Chair's vote decides.
5. The Commission's decision on the award of prizes is final. No appeal lies against it.
6. A protocol is drawn up from the Commission's work containing the names of the prize-winners, the names of the universities, institutes and faculties, the supervisors' names, the titles of the theses, the justification of the choice and the kind and amount of the prizes awarded.
7. The protocol, approved by the Chair of the Competition Commission, is available for inspection by all interested parties.

## VII. PRIZES

### §13

1. The laureates receive diplomas of participation.
2. In both categories (engineering and master's theses) the authors of the three best theses will receive cash and/or in-kind prizes.
3. The Commission reserves the right not to award a prize in a given category and to award a distinction.
4. Prize-winners are entitled to the title of laureate of the Competition for the best engineering and master's theses.
5. The distribution of prizes remains with the Organiser, who reserves the right not to award prizes.
6. Prizes may not be exchanged for any equivalent.
7. The prizes are funded by the SGP national board, the Surveyor General of Poland and sponsors.
8. If the Organiser receives information, before or after the Commission has awarded a prize, that the prize-winning thesis is a plagiarism, or that the law or any provision of these Rules has been infringed in any other way, the Organisers are entitled to:
   1. withhold the transfer or realisation of the prize until the doubts are explained,
   2. refuse to transfer the prize to that participant and transfer it to another participant,
   3. decide not to award a prize in the Competition,
   4. in the case of prizes already awarded — demand the return of the funds and prizes within 30 days of the demand, and, if the demand is not met, bring an action for payment before a court against the laureate.
9. No claims by participants for prizes not awarded to them by the Commission will be considered.
10. The laureates' right to demand the transfer or realisation of the prizes set out in these Rules is inalienable.

## VIII. ANNOUNCEMENT OF RESULTS

### §14

1. Participants and universities will be informed of the results electronically or by telephone, and the list of winners will be published at konkurs.sgp.geodezja.org.pl.
2. The official announcement of the results and the award ceremony will take place at a place and time of which participants will be informed by e-mail or telephone.

## IX. CANCELLATION OF THE COMPETITION

### §15

The Organiser reserves the right to cancel the Competition if:

1. no thesis is submitted,
2. all submitted theses fail to meet the conditions of the Rules,
3. the level of the submitted theses is unsatisfactory.

## X. FINAL AND TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

### §16

1. Entering the Competition is equivalent to accepting these Rules.
2. By entering, a participant acknowledges that:
   1. the names of prize-winning participants, the title of the thesis and a short description (abstract) will be published for promotional purposes connected with the Competition in selected media.
   2. the controller of personal data is the Association of Polish Surveyors.
   3. providing personal data is voluntary, but refusal to provide all of a participant's data excludes participation in the Competition.
   4. the supervisor's lack of consent to the processing and publication of personal data does not disqualify the thesis from the Competition.
   5. the processing of personal data is necessary to conduct the Competition, in particular to communicate with participants, award prizes and carry out promotional activities connected with the Competition.
   6. the participant has the right to access and correct their personal data.
3. Theses entered for the Competition will not be used by the Organiser or by entities acting on its behalf, nor made available to third parties, without the knowledge and consent of the participant.
4. All costs connected with participation are borne by the participants themselves.
5. The Organiser reserves the right to change these Rules.
6. The Organiser is not liable for:
   1. events preventing the proper conduct of the Competition which it could not foresee or prevent, in particular random events including force majeure,
   2. non-delivery of a prize caused by incorrect data (address, account number) given by the laureate,
   3. problems arising from the impossibility of contacting a participant.
7. Detailed information about the Competition is available at konkurs.sgp.geodezja.org.pl.
8. Matters not covered by these Rules are governed by Polish law, including the Civil Code.

### §17

These Rules were adopted by the Presidium of the national board of the Association of Polish Surveyors at its sitting of 1 June 2023.
