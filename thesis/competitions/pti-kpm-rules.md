# Rules of the PTI national competition for the best master's theses in computing

> **Source:** <https://kpm.pti.org.pl/terms> (retrieved 2026-11-01)
> **Organiser on behalf of the PTI national board:** the Lower Silesian Branch of the PTI, Wrocław
> **Conversion:** faithful to the text of the page, with the numbering of the units preserved. English translation 2026-11-07; the Polish original is the source of record, and anything quoted in an application is quoted from it.
> The personal-data notice (§5.7 — an integral part of the Rules) is at the end of the document.

## 1. THE PURPOSE OF THE COMPETITION

The purposes of the competition are:

- raising the level of master's theses in the field of computing,
- exchanging and comparing experience and achievements by presenting the best master's theses,
- promoting the PTI among students and building ties between graduates and the Society

## 2. PARTICIPATION

**2.1.** The competition is open to all graduates of higher-education institutions in Poland (including citizens of other countries) whose theses concern computing and who submitted and defended those theses in the academic year (1 October to 30 September) in which the competition was announced.

**2.2.** A thesis is entered by its author(s), who send, through the form available at kpm.pti.org.pl, the following documents in electronic form:

- a) the thesis,
- b) the questionnaire completed by the author(s), according to the template annexed to these Rules,
- c) a certificate from the university that the thesis entered for the competition was defended as a master's thesis in the academic year in which the competition was announced.

## 3. THE COMPETITION COMMISSION

**3.1.** The PTI national board, on the motion of the President of the Lower Silesian Branch of the PTI in Wrocław, appoints the Competition Commission and its Chair, which assesses the submitted theses.

**3.2.** The Competition Commission has ten members, drawn from:

- a) a representative of the board of the PTI branch,
- b) staff of universities, research institutes and industry.

## 4. CRITERIA AND PROCEDURE OF ASSESSMENT

**4.1.** The Competition Commission assesses the submitted theses on the basis of reviews written by members of the Commission or by additional referees appointed for the purpose.

**4.2.** A review of a competition thesis should contain a justified assessment of:

- a) the topic (aim) of the thesis, the author's justification of it, and its weight for the development of computing,
- b) the solution of the problem,
- c) the author's preparation in the professional knowledge connected with the branch of computing the thesis concerns, and the author's independence in solving theoretical and practical problems,
- d) the possibilities of practical application of the results,
- e) the completeness and selection of the literature,
- f) the editorial level of the thesis: style, linguistic correctness, clarity and conciseness.

**4.3.** Having received reviews of all the theses, the Competition Commission classifies them into prizes and distinctions.

**4.4.** The Commission may withhold any of the prizes, or divide one prize between several theses.

**4.5.** Decisions of the Commission are taken by a majority of the members present at the sitting. In the event of a tie the Chair's vote decides. Decisions are valid provided at least five members took part in the sitting.

**4.6.** The deliberations of the Competition Commission are closed.

## 5. THE COURSE OF THE COMPETITION

**5.1.** The competition is announced at the beginning of the diploma semester. The relevant information is circulated to the PTI student circles, faculty councils and the scientific-didactic councils of institutes, and to the media.

**5.2.** The Competition Commission accepts theses until **10 October** of the calendar year in which the competition was announced.

**5.3.** The results are announced no later than by the end of the calendar year in which the competition was announced.

**5.4.** In addition to the classification described in §4, the Competition Commission may award cash and in-kind prizes to the authors.

**5.5.** After the competition the list of submitted theses (containing the first name, surname and e-mail of the author(s), the title and date of the defence, the name of the university, faculty and institute where the thesis was defended, the academic title and name of the supervisor, and the kind of prize or distinction awarded by the Commission) may be sent, for the purpose of announcing the results, to the PTI national board, the editorial office of the PTI Bulletin, the media, the dean's offices of the faculties and the directors of the institutes from which theses were submitted, and may be published on the website of the PTI, its branches and its student circles.

**5.6.** All participants will be informed of the results. The authors of prize-winning and distinguished theses will be invited in writing to the award ceremony.

**5.7.** An integral part of these Rules is the "Notice on the processing of personal data of competition participants".

## Notice on the processing of personal data

> **Source:** <https://kpm.pti.org.pl/terms-rodo> (retrieved 2026-11-01)

**01.** The controller of personal data is the Polish Information Processing Society (PTI), ul. Solec 38 lok. 103, 00-394 Warsaw, KRS 0000043879, tel. 22 838 47 05, e-mail: pti@pti.org.pl, and through the appointed Data Protection Officer, e-mail: iod@pti.org.pl.

**02.** Personal data are collected for the purpose of taking part in the competition for the best master's theses in computing. They will be processed in order to organise, promote and conduct the competition, including the publication of information about laureates on the websites and social media belonging to the PTI.

**03.** The personal data collected by the PTI are those collected in the registration form at <https://kpm.pti.org.pl/form> and other data provided in the course of the competition through correspondence with the PTI.

**04.** The legal basis for processing participants' data is Art. 6(1)(a) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016, that is, consent to processing given by entering the application through the registration form. Consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out on the basis of consent before its withdrawal.

**05.** Recipients of the personal data indicated in the registration form may be PTI staff, members of the competition commission, entities carrying out tasks commissioned by the PTI under data-processing agreements, and institutions entitled to process data under applicable law.

**06.** Personal data will be processed until consent is withdrawn, taking into account the limitation period of any claims.

**07.** Providing the data is voluntary, but failure to provide it may make participation in the competition impossible.

**08.** The PTI does not intend to transfer participants' personal data to a third country or to international organisations.

**09.** Participants have the right to request access to their data, rectification, portability and erasure, and the right to restrict processing. To exercise these rights, please contact the controller in any convenient form.

**10.** In connection with the processing of personal data by the PTI, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office.
