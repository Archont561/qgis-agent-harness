## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The expressible share moderates the whole analysis: it says how much of a task's requirements reach the interface. If its coding does not pass an agreement test, the moderator claim is indistinguishable from coder bias.

- [ ] #4 The codebook is in the appendix
