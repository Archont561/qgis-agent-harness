A pointer, not a copy. The text lives in `thesis/`; the structure is imposed by §V of the faculty rules.

## Six chapters

| Chapter | Contents | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Introduction | problem, aim, thesis statement, temporal and spatial scope, contributions | written (`thesis/draft/thesis-draft-v0.md` §1) |
| 2. Literature review | set of positions + gap L1-L7 | §2 written as an inventory of currents -> TASK-10 |
| 3. Materials and methods | subject, conditions, equivalence assumptions, measures, taxonomies, corpus, oracle, procedure | §3-§5 of the draft, to be merged -> TASK-13 |
| 4. Results | TSR with CI and MDE, depth curve, `detected_by` distribution | after TASK-12 |
| 5. Discussion | mechanism, limitations, further work | §7 of the draft |
| 6. Summary and conclusions | assessment of the aim, own judgements, proposed solutions | §7.1-7.2 of the draft |

## Outside the chapters

Abstract of at most 4000 characters (written after the results), bibliography from `thesis/literature/literature.bib` converted to the format of Appendix 5, lists of tables and figures, appendices (task cards, parity table, pre-registration, register dump).

## Editorial requirements that are easy to miss

Maps: a linear scale bar or a numeric scale plus a source. Direct quotation: at most 50 words, with a page number. Impersonal form throughout. Every number in chapter 6 has its source in a chapter 4 table.
