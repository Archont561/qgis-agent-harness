Structural resolution: the IMRAD plan has six chapters, because §V requires five parts with the "substantial part" in 2-4 chapters - chapter 3 (Materials and methods) absorbs the instrument and the procedure.

Three non-scientific dependencies that the quality of the work cannot compensate for: the framing in the abstract, the very good grade on the diploma (SGP), and a defence date by about 15 September (PTI and the cartography competition).
