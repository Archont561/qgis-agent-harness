A pointer, not a copy. The schedule follows from external dates; every stage has a milestone in the backlog.

## Stages

| Stage | Milestone | What must be ready |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Settlement and pre-registration | m-0 (M1, G1) | corpus of cards closed, hypotheses and measures written down, versions pinned |
| 2. Instrument and literature | m-1 (M2) | both arm implementations, MCP server, harness, literature review, screening turned into numbers |
| 3. Instrument code | m-5 (M6) | loader, register, CLI, sandbox; parity confirmed by a test |
| 4. Main series | m-2 (M3) | the main series, results in chapter 4 |
| 5. Text and formalities | m-3 (M4) | §3-§6 merged, abstract, Appendix 5 format, submission to APD |
| 6. Defence and competitions | m-4 (M5) | defence, 70 x 100 poster, two applications |

## Dates that drive everything else

- **A defence by about 15 September** opens all four competitions; a defence after 25 September loses PTI and the seventeenth competition.
- **Thesis to APD by 30 September**, PTI on 10 October, the seventeenth competition around 25 September.
- The grade on the diploma enters SGP eligibility without exception - the quality of the work cannot compensate for it.

## Rules of order

The pre-registration closes before the first run of the main series; after G1 a change of measure voids the series, it does not merely require a footnote. Chapter 4 is written from the generated tables, not from memory. Formalities (abstract, declarations, software) start together with chapter 6, not after it.
