A pointer, not a copy. Full text: `thesis/draft/structure.md`.

The metaplan gives the introduction four paragraphs with linking sentences (how it is -> why it is so -> how it should be -> what to do). IMRAD gives the skeleton: I = chapters 1-2, M = chapter 3, R = chapter 4, D = chapters 5-6. Entailment check: every number in chapter 6 has its source in a chapter 4 table, and every chapter 4 table has its counterpart in the measures of chapter 3.
